🔬 What does this explore?

Target concept: working memory and long-term memory retrieval within information processing.

  • Encoding: noticing the symbols and their locations.
  • Retention: keeping recently seen locations in mind.
  • Retrieval: remembering where a matching symbol appeared.
  • Strategy: using location patterns or grouping to support recall.
No diagnosisLearning activity only

📝 Observe like a psychologist

After the game, ask the player:

  • What strategy did you use to remember locations?
  • Did any pattern or distraction affect your choices?
  • What would you change if you played again?

Record only a participant code, moves, time, accuracy and brief observations. Do not compare people as “good” or “bad” at memory.

Designed for a classroom demonstration • Adapt the pace and instructions to each participant • Participation is voluntary
